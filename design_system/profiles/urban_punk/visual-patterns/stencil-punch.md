# Pattern: Stencil Punch (Urban Punk)

- **ID**: `stencil_punch`
- **Profile**: `urban_punk`
- **Purpose**: High-energy opening hook or conclusion statement combining uppercase impact headline with tape badge eyebrow.

## Composition
1. Uppercase badge rotated -1.5deg with acid lime background (`#ccff00`) and black mono text.
2. Heavy headline (`Impact`, 72px–96px) in photocopy white.
3. Rapid snap transition (`--motion-focus: 280ms`, `--ease-snap`).
