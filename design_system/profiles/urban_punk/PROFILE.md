# Visual Profile: Urban Punk

- **Identifier**: `urban_punk`
- **Category**: Street / Poster / Irreverent Commentary
- **Tone**: Adult, high-contrast, kinetic, provocative, razor-sharp.

## Design Direction

Urban Punk draws on the visual energy of street wheatpaste posters, photocopied fanzines, stencils, and industrial signage.

It delivers immediate, high-impact confrontation for topics demanding skepticism, frustration, and uncompromising clarity.

## Visual Grammar

- **Typography**: Dense, heavy woodcut/grotesque headlines (`Impact`, `Arial Black`) providing instant visual weight, paired with crisp, highly legible neutral sans-serif body copy and industrial monospace metadata.
- **Color Palette**: Deep asphalt ground (`#0a0a0c`), raw photocopy white (`#f8fafc`), high-voltage acid lime (`#ccff00`), punch neon pink (`#ff2a6d`), and industrial hazard orange (`#ff6b00`).
- **Paste-up Logic**: Hard drop shadows, raw cut edges, diagonal tape accents, and sticker-style labels.
- **Motion Tendencies**: Fast punch-in cuts, snappy state transitions, kinetic reveals, and abrupt visual freezes before punchlines.

## Critical Discipline (What to Avoid)

- **Not an Intensity Slider**: Urban Punk is an independent aesthetic language, not an extreme version of Editorial Grunge. Do not treat "punk" as a percentage dial.
- **Avoid Caricature**: No cartoonish graffiti tags, illegible bubble letters, childish scribbles, or chaotic decorative noise that destroys legibility.
- **Reasoning First**: The visual energy amplifies strong reasoning; it does not substitute for it.
