# Visual Profile: Editorial Grunge

- **Identifier**: `editorial_grunge`
- **Category**: Investigative / Analytical Commentary
- **Tone**: Adult, intelligent, urban, evidence-first.

## Design Direction

Editorial Grunge prioritizes sharp, disciplined information design with subtle distressed and analog textures as an ingredient, never a costume.

It is tailored for:
- policy analysis;
- investigative journalism breakdowns;
- economics and systemic commentary;
- evidence-heavy cultural debates.

## Visual Grammar

- **Typography**: Authoritative editorial serif headlines paired with teletype/typewriter monospace labels and clean, legible sans-serif body copy.
- **Color Palette**: Dark slate newsroom backgrounds or warm archival newsprint surfaces, printer's ink black, highlighter yellow (`#eab308`), investigative crimson (`#dc2626`), and muted paper tones.
- **Texture**: Halftone dot screens, subtle photocopier grain, ruled newsprint borders, and archival document stamps. Texture supports the evidence; it never obscures legibility.
- **Motion Tendencies**: Deliberate editorial cuts, firm documentary reveals, stamp impacts, and generous inspection holds (`--hold-inspect`, `--hold-think`).

## Anti-Patterns (What to Avoid)

- Novelty graffiti fonts or street caricature.
- Cartoonish splatter or decorative grunge clutter that distracts from the argument.
- Fake-edgy visual noise with weak underlying evidence.
- Generic influencer gradients, rounded pastel bubbles, or corporate polish.
