# Pattern: Headline Takeover (Editorial Grunge)

- **ID**: `headline_takeover`
- **Profile**: `editorial_grunge`
- **Purpose**: Creates massive editorial impact using a dominant serif statement that yields to archival evidence.

## Composition
1. Eyebrow in uppercase typewriter mono (`var(--font-mono)`).
2. Giant serif headline (`var(--font-headline)`, 72px–96px) with selective highlighter accent (`#eab308`).
3. Teletype evidence badge grounding the claim.
